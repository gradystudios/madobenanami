Why Touhou Project?
Click to Expand
I guess this was technically already went over, but.. When I was younger, I became attached for how diverse of a franchise it really is. In characters, music, story, and all the fanwork that revolves around it. It's such a unique franchise in that regard, and it's nice to have a series that will always be creative in every way, even when my creativity and passion dies with age. It's just so easy to get attached to the setting and characters, you can meet a hundred fans all with a unique favorite character. There's nothing like it. nothing at all. I still remember my first TRUE full-on exposure, though I had some prior experience with characters and music and the such from it. It was this odd Nico Nico reuploaded Ting Tang Walla Walla Bing Bang (witch doctor) vid. I miss the web. I'm pretty sure it's still available somewhere. I'd blame that for me really delving deep into "Who was the girl with purple bows riding something?". I'm sure by then I had a more explicit understanding though.
